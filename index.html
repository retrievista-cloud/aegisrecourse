SIM-Swap Attack Prevention

Security · March 2026 · 8 min read

SIM-swap fraud has surged over 300% in 2025 to 2026. Attackers specifically target crypto holders using social engineering against mobile carriers.

What is a SIM-Swap Attack?

An attacker convinces your mobile carrier to transfer your phone number to a SIM they control. With your number, they bypass SMS-based 2FA on exchange accounts and email, enabling full account takeover within minutes.

Defensive Steps That Actually Work

  • Set a SIM Lock PIN with Your Carrier — a mandatory PIN required for any account changes
  • Switch to App-Based 2FA — replace SMS 2FA with Google Authenticator or a YubiKey
  • Use a Dedicated Email for Crypto — secured with a hardware key, used only for exchange accounts
  • Remove Your Phone Number from Exchange Accounts — use authenticator apps instead

Partial Seed Phrase Recovery

Recovery · February 2026 · 10 min read

Losing even a few words of your seed phrase does not necessarily mean permanent loss. With enough known words, cryptographic reconstruction may be possible.

How Partial Recovery Works

BIP39 seed phrases include a checksum meaning not all word combinations are valid, dramatically reducing combinations to test when some words are missing.

  • 12-word phrase, 1 missing word — 2,048 combinations, recoverable in seconds
  • 12-word phrase, 2 missing words — approximately 4 million combinations, minutes with our GPU cluster
  • 24-word phrase, 3 missing words — approximately 8.5 billion valid combinations, hours to days

Warning

Never enter your partial seed phrase into any online tool. Scammers specifically target people who have lost seed phrase words. Always verify any recovery service before sharing wallet information.
